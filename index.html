Y — Process

How a project moves.

C01

Discover

Audience, market, and competitive landscape — understanding what the work actually needs to do before it looks like anything.

M02

Define

Strategy and positioning distilled into a clear creative direction, agreed on before a single pixel is final.

Y03

Design

Exploration, critique, and refinement in tight loops — usually three rounds, rarely more than five.

K04

Deliver

Production-ready files, documented guidelines, and a handoff that doesn't need a follow-up call to decode.
